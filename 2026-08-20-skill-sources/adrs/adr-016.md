# ADR-016: Pre-overlay candidate projection with opaque stable root identity

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-08-20

## Context

Business Rule 12 and US-008.EC-1 promise that same-named skills from different sources stay distinguishable and invocable through qualified forms. Peer review round 1 (B-005) showed the original design could not deliver it: the name-keyed last-writer-wins overlay discards shadowed definitions from the effective registry, so only the winner could ever be projected as a command; and `Source.ID = display slug` is not a stable identity (two custom roots can share a basename, and list-position suffixes change on reorder), which would break the `sameSkillSource` drift check between catalog build and expansion.

## Decision

- The session command catalog builds from a **pre-overlay candidate projection**: candidates keyed by `RootID` + physical skill identity (realpath), computed before the name overlay. The effective winner projects the bare command; every eligible same-named candidate projects a qualified command.
- **`RootID` is the identity** for command sources, cache keys, exposure attribution, and drift checks: an opaque value derived **only** from stable ownership + location fields — owner scope, canonical workspace id, root kind, and canonical dir (round 2, B-004: the display slug is excluded — collision re-allocation may change it, and identity must not move when presentation does). Display slugs (`agents`, `claude`, `team-skills`) remain purely presentational (picker chips, ORIGIN columns, qualified-token display form).
- Custom display slugs are allocated by a batch, order-independent allocator: sanitized basename; collisions get a deterministic short hash of the canonical path. Guarantees (round 3, N-002): reordering never changes any slug; adding or removing a **non-colliding** path changes no other slug; introducing a new basename collision may re-suffix the previously bare holder of that basename — which is exactly why display slugs are presentational and `RootID` (slug-free) is the identity that never moves.
- Expansion resolves the exact candidate by `RootID` and revalidates generation, source, and content before injecting.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Winner-only projection (original design)

- **Why rejected**: Shadowed homonyms become uninvocable — directly contradicts BR-12/US-008.EC-1 (the owner's accepted behavior).

### Alternative 2: Display slug as identity with positional suffixes

- **Why rejected**: Not unique across scopes, not stable under config reordering; drift checks would reject valid invocations or accept wrong ones.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Qualified invocation works for every candidate; identity survives config edits; realpath dedup still collapses the same physical skill reached via multiple roots (aliases are one candidate, homonyms are distinct candidates).

### Negative

- The catalog projection carries more entries than the effective registry — bounded by real homonym counts (small in practice).

### Risks

- Token-form ambiguity when two candidates share a display slug — impossible by construction (slug collisions get hash suffixes; RootID disambiguates internally).

## Implementation Notes

Candidates only include definitions that would be legal winners (enabled, verified, active); a candidate whose root leaves the config drops from the projection at the next generation, and in-flight invocations fail through the existing drift rejection.
