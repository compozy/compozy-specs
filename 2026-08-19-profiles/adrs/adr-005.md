# ADR-005: "All profiles" is a switcher state that creates into `default`

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-08-20 (decided in grill-me; chip form reconfirmed in spec round 2)

## Context

With work scoped per profile, the operator still needs a whole-machine view ("how do I list sessions from all profiles?"). A read-only aggregate was considered and rejected by the operator because gating every creation surface "would generate a LOT of UI modification". Market evidence cuts both ways: Slack retreated from a unified view partly because creation context became unclear; Notion's silent default-filing is the documented misfiling trap.

## Decision

- The aggregate lives as a **state of the profile switcher** (not a third menubar control). Rows are always owner-labeled; archived owners included.
- **Creating while in "All profiles" creates in `default`.** Guardrails at the shared creation primitives only: a fixed, always-visible destination chip ("→ default") and a success toast naming the owner. The chip is a label, **not** a picker (reconfirmed: a picker would ripple through too many UI surfaces).
- The daemon has exactly **two read modes**: scoped-to-one-profile (the default of every surface) and explicit labeled aggregate — used by the All state, the terminal's all-profiles listing option, and machine-wide observe/usage dashboards. No third mode.
- Axes compose: profile × Global toggle × workspace.
- The aggregate is never remembered as a workspace's selection (ADR-003) — returning always lands on a real profile.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Read-only aggregate

- **Description**: "All profiles" disables creation everywhere.
- **Pros**: no misfiling possible.
- **Cons**: every creation surface needs a disabled state + explanation; heavy UI sweep.
- **Why rejected**: operator decision (cost); the calm alternative (label + toast) covers the risk.

### Alternative 2: Destination picker at creation (Slack's pattern)

- **Description**: chip preselects `default` but is changeable inline.
- **Pros**: matches market mitigation for misfiling.
- **Cons**: turns every shared creation primitive into a scope-aware control; large UI ripple.
- **Why rejected**: operator decision in spec round 2 (Q6) — fixed label wins.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Zero read-only sweep; two read modes keep the daemon contract small; audit flows compose with Global/workspace axes.

### Negative

- Misfiling into `default` remains possible when the operator ignores the chip — accepted; the owner-naming toast surfaces it immediately, and recovery is "switch and recreate" until a transfer verb exists (future scope).

### Risks

- Chip too subtle → it must be part of the default read of creation surfaces (a `_uiux.md` requirement), not a tooltip.
