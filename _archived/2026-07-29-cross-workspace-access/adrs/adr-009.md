# ADR-009: Native workspace authority is classified once

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-07-29

## Context

The native workspace binder and authorizer classified trusted callers differently. A deep-review fix
correctly denied identified sessions using global/all scope, but two follow-up patches exposed the
duplicated rule: unbound daemon calls were denied during binding even though authorization already
treated them as trusted. The repository two-touch rule requires a structural decision before another
production change.

## Decision

One helper classifies native workspace caller authority for both binding and authorization. Explicit
operator scopes and fully unbound daemon scopes are trusted. Any identified scope is not trusted for
global/all access, including when it supplies its home workspace explicitly.

Global/all authorization precedes workspace injection. Trusted global calls remain unbound;
identified global calls fail closed. Non-global workspace resolution and policy evaluation remain
unchanged.

## Consequences

- Binder and authorizer cannot drift on operator/daemon classification.
- Valid daemon global operations reach their owning input and service validation.
- Identified sessions retain the round-2 global/all isolation fix.
- The accepted ADR-006 headerless-operator beta posture remains explicit; this ADR adds no technical
  operator authentication.
