# ADR-002: One agent-facing call verb; the spawn surface is deleted

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-08-20

## Context

`session_spawn` today is governed session creation, not a call: no prompt, no return slot, and an `idempotency_key` that is accepted and ignored (`internal/session/spawn.go:207`; asserted non-idempotent by `spawn_test.go:327`). Adding a call verb *next to* spawn would give agents two overlapping verbs and a choice they routinely get wrong (tool-choice confusion). No market reference separates "create worker" from "call worker" on the agent-facing surface: omp `task`, hermes `delegate_task`, Codex `spawn_agent` all carry the first prompt in the spawn.

## Decision

The agent-facing surface exposes **exactly one delegation verb: the call**, with spawn-or-address semantics — it spawns a governed child when the target does not exist and addresses the existing child (live or parked) when it does. First call and follow-up use the same verb. The call carries the prompt, the expected-result schema, and the spawn governance parameters (TTL, permission narrowing atoms). The entire agent-facing spawn surface is **deleted**: `compozy__session_spawn` native tool, `compozy spawn` CLI verb, the HTTP/UDS spawn route and contract types, and the inert `idempotency_key` input. Idempotency becomes real on the call (caller-chosen key). The internal Go spawn machinery (governance, permission subset validation, lineage, caps) survives as the call's engine and keeps serving internal roles (memory-extractor, auto-title) through internal paths.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Extend `session_spawn` with `prompt` + `expect`

- **Description**: Spawn becomes the call.
- **Pros**: Smallest diff; matches omp/Codex shape.
- **Cons**: Follow-up to a live/parked child is not a spawn, so a second verb appears anyway; keeps a verb whose name describes creation, not delegation.
- **Why rejected**: Re-creates the two-verb confusion one release later.

### Alternative 2: New call verb, keep `session_spawn` exposed

- **Description**: Call for delegation; spawn stays for bare creation.
- **Pros**: Preserves a bare-creation escape hatch.
- **Cons**: Two overlapping agent-facing verbs; no evidenced agent-facing use of bare creation (internal roles use the Go path, not the tool).
- **Why rejected**: Operator's call: two verbs generate agent confusion about which to use; the bare-creation surface has no real consumer.

## Consequences

### Positive

- One verb to document, gate, and reason about; small tool-token footprint (the Network 18-tool weight is the cautionary tale).
- The inert-input defect is removed by deletion, honoring the hard-cut policy (SD-002).

### Negative

- Breaking change with a wide delete list (tool, toolset registration, CLI, HTTP/UDS contract, generated TS, docs, skills references).

### Risks

- A future need for prompt-less child creation — reintroduce deliberately as a call option, not by resurrecting the old verb.

## Implementation Notes

Delete targets enumerated in `_spec.md` Impact Analysis. Verb naming is decided in the `_dx.md` surface grill (do **not** name it "handoff" — market vocabulary reserves that for transfer-of-control).
