# ADR-009: Calls are asynchronous by default; the result arrives as a result-carrying wake; await is an explicit bounded verb

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-08-20

## Context

`session_prompt` today blocks the caller's tool call for the target's entire turn and discards the response stream — the canonical lesson against synchronous cross-session transport. hermes shipped and then deprecated a model-facing `background` flag ("the model does not choose" — models choose badly); herdr fuses submit-and-wait into one request; Codex `wait_agent` is an activity gate. ADR-004 already makes the completion wake carry the typed result reference.

## Decision

The call verb **returns immediately** with the call identity and state; the caller continues its turn or ends it. Completion reaches the caller as a **non-interrupting wake that carries the typed result** (ADR-004) — no polling, no result-less completion signal. For flows that need the result inline, an **explicit await verb** exists: bounded, resumable (the badge-wait skeleton: register-before-snapshot, pinned identity, resume id), returning the typed result or a typed timeout. There is **no model-facing sync/async flag on the call itself**. Loop nodes keep their coordinator-managed await semantics.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Blocking by default

- **Description**: Call holds the caller's turn until the child finishes (with timeout).
- **Pros**: Simplest mental model.
- **Cons**: Repeats the `session_prompt` lesson; a parent pinned for a child's whole run; kills cheap fan-out.
- **Why rejected**: Structurally wrong for long-running children.

### Alternative 2: Per-call sync/async flag

- **Description**: Model chooses per call.
- **Pros**: Flexibility.
- **Cons**: hermes tried exactly this and deprecated it — models choose badly and then rationalize.
- **Why rejected**: The runtime owns the posture; an explicit await verb covers the inline case deliberately.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Fan-out is cheap by construction; parents never hang on children.
- One wait skeleton (badge wait) serves both badge waits and result awaits.

### Negative

- Two verbs (call + await) instead of one — justified because await is also the follow-up/reattach surface after restarts.

### Risks

- Callers that end their turn without awaiting rely entirely on wake delivery — mitigated by durable delivery + parked-revive (ADR-003) and the result being fetchable by call id at any time.
